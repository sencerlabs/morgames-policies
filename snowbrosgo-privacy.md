# SnowBrosGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

SnowBrosGo (Android package ai.moruk.snowbrosgo) is an offline single-player arcade game provided by Moruk LLC. Contact: support@moruk.co.

## Data collection and sharing
The game does not collect or send personal data to Moruk LLC or third parties. It does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, account sign-in, in-app purchases, cloud saves, chat, or social sharing. It does not request access to contacts, location, microphone, camera, photos, or device identifiers. This version does not request Internet permission.

## Information stored on your device
The game stores your best score, unlocked stage, princess-rescue progress, and sound preference in private app storage on your device. This information is used only to continue your game and remember your preferences. It is not uploaded or shared. Android app backup is disabled for this game. Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game deletes these saved settings and progress.

## Children
The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players. It has no advertising, account registration, or communication features.

## External services and support
Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own privacy policies when distributing the game or providing system services. If you choose to email support@moruk.co, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily include. We use it only to answer your request and retain it only as needed for that request or applicable obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

## Security and retention
Game progress remains in private local app storage until you clear it or uninstall the game. We do not operate a server holding your gameplay data. Access to support correspondence is limited to handling support requests.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the effective date and the bundled policy will be updated. Material changes to data practices will be disclosed before they take effect.
