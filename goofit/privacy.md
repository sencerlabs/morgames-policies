## English (en)

Privacy notice

GooFit · Operator: Moruk LLC · Contact: support@moruk.co

This version has no account, advertising or analytics service. Language, gender, goal, level, equipment, theme and audio preferences, favourites, set weights/repetitions, times and workout history are stored on your phone to build plans, show progress and resume sessions.

When you start a walk/run and grant location permission, Android and Google Play location services process location to calculate distance and speed, including while the screen is off during tracking. GooFit does not save route coordinates to files; it saves distance, time and activity summaries. You can disable permission in phone settings; other workouts do not require location. Platform services have their own privacy terms.

Coaching uses an installed offline voice. This version does not send profiles or workout records to a GooFit server. An exported backup goes to the app/location you choose, under that service’s terms. Information you email may be processed by email providers.

Records remain until you delete them or uninstall. In Profile you can export records or delete all app data; delete exported copies separately. Confirmation date, document version and language stay on this device and are excluded from workout backups. This notice is not consent to advertising or processing health data. Contact support@moruk.co for information, correction, deletion and other applicable data rights.
