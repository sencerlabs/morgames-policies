Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the StepTracking Android app, presented under the StepX brand ("the App"), handles information. The App does not require an account and does not operate its own account or cloud-sync service.

Information stored on your device

The App reads step-counter or step-detector sensor data to count steps. It calculates activity time, distance and calorie estimates. Your goals, measurement preferences, weight and height entries, water entries, step history and heart-rate estimates may be stored in the App's private storage on your device. These values are used to provide the App's features and are not sent to a StepTracking server.

You can clear this information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Android device backup or transfer features may handle app data according to your device and Android backup settings.

Camera-based heart-rate estimate

If you choose to use the optional heart-rate feature, the App uses the rear camera and flash to analyze changes in light while your fingertip covers them. Image frames are analyzed temporarily in memory to calculate an estimate; the App does not save or upload those frames. The resulting estimate may be saved in the App's private storage on your device. This is an approximate wellness feature, not a medical measurement or diagnosis.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or App Set ID. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK transmits data to Google under its own privacy and security practices.

Learn more in Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage your Android advertising ID and related ad privacy settings in your device settings where available.

Permissions and notifications

Physical activity permission is used for step counting. Camera permission is requested only when you open the optional heart-rate estimate. Notification permission, where required by Android, is used for reminders and ongoing step-counting notifications. The App may start its step-counting service after a device restart if tracking was enabled. The App does not request location, contacts or microphone access.

Sharing, retention and deletion

StepTracking does not sell your information. Information processed by the App's features remains in its private device storage, subject to Android backup settings. Advertising information is handled by Google Mobile Ads as described above and may be retained by Google under its policies. You can delete locally stored App data through Android Settings → Apps → StepTracking → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App. To manage information collected by Google for advertising, use Google's privacy controls or your device's ad privacy settings.

Children

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13, and StepTracking does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its own servers. The App includes a third-party advertising SDK; its data practices are described above. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information to us can contact us using the address below.

Security

App feature data is kept in the App's private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google's handling of advertising data is governed by Google's own safeguards and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact sport@moruk.co.
